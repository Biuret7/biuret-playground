Made for a realistic routine.

StudyFlow divides work around your daily time, keeps prerequisite order and builds in review. The desktop application stores your plans and reflections locally; the English CLI uses the same engine.

This is a planning preview, not the desktop interface. It computes inside your browser without saving to your desktop database. Learning completion is self-assessed; this tool does not award certificates.

INTERACTIVE PREVIEW

Make room for learning.

Choose a template or your own topics. Set a routine and an optional target date.

One unique topic per line, up to 8 topics. Add prerequisite topics first. Your text stays as entered in both languages.

We check whether your routine fits; we do not shorten learning to promise a deadline.

Study days

Changing only the target keeps progress. Changing topics, level, daily time or study days creates a fresh plan; an existing saved plan stays available in My Work.

The six templates follow the Python planning rules. Custom goals and rescheduling extend this browser edition. No external AI or account; device saving is optional.
